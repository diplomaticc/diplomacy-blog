### Briefing Overview
In the wake of severe kinetic escalations and targeted security operations across the theater, backchannel communications have successfully produced a tentative 60-day truce expansion agreement between Washington and Tehran from May 27–28, 2026.

### Strategic Provisions & Parameters

*   **The Strait of Hormuz:** Immediate guidelines require the immediate suspension of maritime blockades across Iranian commercial ports, restoring verified, unrestricted shipping transit corridors throughout the Persian Gulf.
*   **Nuclear Constraints:** Under temporary framework conditions, Tehran must completely halt highly enriched uranium processing vectors and commit to verified stockpile containment protocols.
*   **Diplomatic Timeline:** The agreed-upon 60-day window sets a strict 30-to-60 day launch schedule for formal, multilateral atomic treaty negotiations.
*   **Pakistani Backchannel Mediation:** Secure diplomatic coordination and logistical relays were completely facilitated via Pakistani diplomatic intermediaries acting as primary non-aligned emissaries.
*   **UN Security Council Integration:** Upon receiving final executive approval from the White House within the 60-day stabilization window, the memorandum will be advanced to the UN Security Council to be codified as a legally binding international resolution.
