Bilateral interbank clearing networks are being developed to streamline cryptographic transactional settlement protocols between regions. This next-generation infrastructure aims to bypass traditional correspondent banking delays, reduce settlement time from days to seconds, and establish 24/7 borderless settlement with no weekend or holiday payment gaps.

**Real-World Initiatives Underway:**
* **Project mBridge:** BIS + Central Banks (MVP live since mid-2024)
* **Cross-Border CBDC Pilots:** Reserve Bank of India (Targeting 2026–2027)
* **Digital Euro Pilot:** European Central Bank (Expected Mid-2027)
* **SWIFT CBDC Platform:** SWIFT Institute (Next-generation connectivity platform)

**Vision:** Enabling seamless, cryptographic settlement between central bank digital currencies—reducing reliance on legacy messaging systems and enhancing direct trade liquidity across borders.
